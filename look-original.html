01Practice

A small practice, working throughout New Zealand

We are a small firm doing both small and large work throughout New Zealand. Nott Architects is an architecture and design practice based at 34 Allen Street in Christchurch, on New Zealand's South Island.

We are an accredited New Zealand Institute of Architects Practice. Our work has been recognised with NZIA regional awards in architecture, interior architecture, hospitality, sustainability and colour, and more recently with a NZIA national award for hospitality.

Whether your project is a small one or a large one, the best way to start is a conversation. Call the studio on 03 377 9075 or write to charlie@nott.co.nz.

Where do Nott Architects work?

The environments we work in are as diverse as the projects; from coastal, rural and alpine to urban; often in areas of outstanding natural landscape.

From our Christchurch studio we take on projects across New Zealand. The setting of each project is as varied as the brief itself, and much of our work sits within some of the country's most remarkable landscapes.

04FAQ

Questions clients ask

What size of project do you take on?

Both. We are a small firm doing both small and large work, so whatever the scale of your project, we're happy to talk it through.

Do you work outside Christchurch?

Yes. Our studio is in Christchurch, and we work throughout New Zealand in coastal, rural, alpine and urban environments.

Is Nott Architects an accredited practice?

Yes. We are an accredited New Zealand Institute of Architects (NZIA) Practice.

How do I start a conversation about my project?
